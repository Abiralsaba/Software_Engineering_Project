Guidelines & FAQs

নির্দেশিকা ও সাধারণ প্রশ্নাবলী

Important information about e-Passport application process

Required Documents for New Passport
  • National ID Card (NID): Original + photocopy (both sides). 10 or 17 digit NID number required.
  • Online Birth Certificate: 17-digit birth registration number. Must be from BDRIS (bdris.gov.bd).
  • Passport-size Photographs: Recent color photo with white background, size 35mm × 45mm. Must be taken within last 6 months.
  • Academic Certificate: Optional, but recommended for profession verification.
  • Citizenship Certificate: Required if born before NID era or for special cases.
Documents for Renewal
  • Previous Passport: Original passport + photocopy of all pages (including blank pages).
  • National ID Card (NID): Original + photocopy.
  • Online Birth Certificate: 17-digit registration number.
  • Passport-size Photographs: Same specifications as new passport.
  • Note: Apply for renewal at least 6 months before passport expiry date.
Documents for Lost/Damaged Passport
  • General Diary (GD): Must be filed at nearest police station within 24 hours of loss.
  • Court Affidavit: Sworn statement on judicial stamp paper describing circumstances of loss.
  • National ID Card (NID): Original + photocopy.
  • Online Birth Certificate: 17-digit registration number.
  • Photocopy of Lost Passport: If available, any photocopy of the lost passport.
  • Penalty Fee: Additional ৳5,000 penalty applies for lost/damaged replacement.
For Government Employees
  • NOC (No Objection Certificate): From controlling authority/ministry. Mandatory for all government employees.
  • Service ID Card: Copy of government service identity card.
  • Government Order (GO): Required for Official/Diplomatic passport applications.
  • Ministry Forwarding Letter: Required for Official/Diplomatic passport.
  • All standard documents for ordinary passport are also required.
Photo Specifications
  • Size: 35mm × 45mm (width × height)
  • Background: Plain white background with no shadow
  • Face coverage: Face must cover 70-80% of the photograph
  • Expression: Neutral expression, mouth closed, both eyes open
  • Glasses: No glasses or sunglasses
  • Head covering: Religious head coverings allowed if face is fully visible
  • Recency: Must be taken within the last 6 months
  • Quality: High resolution, no blur, proper lighting, no red-eye
Application Process Steps
  • Step 1 — Online Application: Fill out the online form with all required information.
  • Step 2 — Fee Payment: Pay the applicable fee via Sonali Bank Challan, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or VISA/MasterCard.
  • Step 3 — Biometric Enrollment: Visit your selected Regional Passport Office on the scheduled date for biometric capture (fingerprint, iris scan, facial photo).
  • Step 4 — Police Verification: Special Branch police will verify your provided information at your permanent address.
  • Step 5 — Processing: Your application will be processed, passport printed and quality checked.
  • Step 6 — Delivery: Collect your e-Passport from the Regional Passport Office you selected, or receive it via postal delivery.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
  • Q: How long does it take to get an e-Passport?
    A: Regular delivery takes 15-21 working days, Express 7 working days, and Super Express 2-3 working days after biometric enrollment.
  • Q: Can I apply for someone else?
    A: No, you must apply in person. For minors, parents/legal guardians can apply.
  • Q: What if my NID information doesn't match my birth certificate?
    A: You must first correct the information in either document before applying. Contact NID wing or BDRIS.
  • Q: Can I have two valid passports at the same time?
    A: No. When you apply for a new passport, the old one gets cancelled.
  • Q: What is the validity of an e-Passport?
    A: e-Passports are available in 5-year and 10-year validity options.
  • Q: Can I track my passport delivery?
    A: Yes, use the Track Application section with your application number to check real-time status.

Important Notice

📌 All passport applications are processed by the Department of Immigration & Passports (DIP), Government of Bangladesh.

📌 Providing false information is punishable under Passport Act, 2023.

📌 Biometric enrollment (fingerprint + iris scan) is mandatory at the Regional Passport Office.

📌 Police verification is conducted by the Special Branch at your permanent address.

📌 For any queries, contact DIP helpline: 16345

📌 Official website: www.dip.gov.bd
